I’m a software engineer in Cincinnati building web apps for clinical research, with Angular on the front end and .NET behind it. I’ve been doing it for three years.

Things I’ve built

  • Clinical trial pre-screening

    A web app research sites use to log potential participants, track recruitment and report on pre-screening results. I’ve worked across the front end and services since early 2024: patient views, custom search, imports, SSRS reporting and the email notifications.

  • Site contacts and an Outlook add-in

    A system for recording communications with research sites, including an Outlook add-in that files emails against the right contact as they’re sent. I got the add-in ready for production, made the data imports safe to re-run, and ran its releases.

  • Enrollment projections

    A tool for forecasting study enrollment and comparing scenarios side by side. I reworked the scenario builder, added a comparison page with graphs and export, and fixed a calculation bug that was producing wrong projections.
